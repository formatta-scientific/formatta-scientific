SERVICE 01

Journal Formatting

SERVICE 02

References & Citations Check

From $30 USD

Rigorous cross-verification between in-text citations and reference list entries to eliminate bibliographic errors prior to submission.

Inclusions:
  • Cross-checking all in-text citations against the reference list and vice-versa
  • Identifying missing, orphaned, duplicate, or inconsistent reference entries
  • Verifying author names, publication year, title, journal name, volume & issue
  • Locating and appending verified DOIs via Crossref metadata
  • Ordering and formatting according to specified citation style
Exclusions:
  • Evaluating if cited sources factually or scientifically support textual claims
  • Locating replacement papers for invalidly cited sources

  • Ambiguous passages flagged in a separate decision report
Exclusions:
  • Full translation from foreign languages
  • Developmental/Scientific Editing or methodological rewriting
